EVIDENCE & METHODOLOGY

A study with visible assumptions.

This app depicts the 1 November 2026 Learjet 60 N722BD accident near Greenacres in Palm Beach County. The investigation is ongoing. It does not establish the accident’s cause.

Documented observations

Flightradar24 reports a right-hand descent and a final received altitude of 8,750 ft at 17:49:22 UTC; vertical rate exceeded 32,000 ft/min. The crew reported icing, turbulence and precipitation.

The nearby airport’s archived KDJT 17:53Z METAR reports 08016G28KT, 10SM, −RA, SCT026 SCT050 OVC060, 26/23, A3006. It was issued four minutes after the reported crash minute; surface conditions do not measure conditions along the jet’s path.

ATC reporting reviewed by CNN/WPTV describes −47°C and moderate turbulence with altitude deviations up to 700 ft.

What this app models

The custom profile starts at 40,000 ft / 100 mph and reaches 800 mph at ground impact, as requested. These are custom scenario endpoints, not verified accident speeds. Speed means modeled true airspeed. The path is integrated from that speed and flight-path angle.

The published-track option uses downloaded ADS-B positions, barometric altitude and groundspeed. After the last changing-altitude sample at 8,750 ft, an explicitly marked inferred segment continues to the approximate impact site. Its vertical motion assumes gravity without drag or lift; horizontal motion interpolates to the site. Pitch is inferred from motion; bank is illustrative. Flight levels are visualized as approximate MSL heights.

Cloud density, vertical extent, icing appearance, visibility inside clouds and turbulence motion are illustrative. Engine indications, controls and avionics layout are decorative. No flight-recorder attitude, upper-air wind profile or authenticated cockpit reconstruction is available here. Satellite imagery is a cloud-free 2024 mosaic, not event-time photography.

Speed & descent timing

The main speed readout for ADS-B is estimated total 3D motion: horizontal groundspeed combined with barometric vertical speed. Horizontal GS remains on the navigation display. This is not measured true airspeed. The inferred tail continues accelerating vertically to impact.

The granular feed reaches 35,450 ft at 17:48:14.875Z and 8,750 ft at 17:49:22.955Z: 68.08 seconds. Impact is not measured in these data. The peak plateau lasts until 17:48:17.798Z. This app adds about 10.25 seconds after the last altitude sample, yielding approximately 75.45 seconds from the end of the climb to modeled impact (78.37 seconds from the first peak report). The 32-second onset gap in the standard CSV is filled using granular reports.

Buildings & radio alignment

Buildings use 4,951 current OpenStreetMap footprints around the site, with partial coverage. Most heights are an assumed 4.5 m; eight mapped floor counts use an assumed 3 m per floor. Ground is a flat 16 ft MSL approximation, with no building collision model.

ATC and sound are enabled by default and start when you press Play. The ATC button controls the original radio audio from the video quoted in the supplied X post. Impact is aligned to audio 1:45.65, approximately 0.35 seconds after the final phrase ends. This is editorial timing, not a verified UTC correlation. Seeking, pausing and playback rate apply to the audio. The longer profile has leading silence; the shorter profile begins partway through the clip.
